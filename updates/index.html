Updates

This space will contain project news and workshop announcements relating to the Prototyping a Plural Archive of Care project.

First workshop

A participatory, drop-in public workshop that explores how care is felt, practiced, and imagined across familial, community, and institutional settings — past, present, and future — is being planned at a community-friendly, accessible space in central Oxford. The workshop is planned to coincide with Time to Talk Day to raise awareness of mental health.

Active research

Vincent Straub, Mehrunisha Suleman, and Deshna Shah are engaged in research that will inform the project, including work on the ethical dimensions of health inequalities, how invisible systems shape perception, identity, and consciousness, and the potential and governance of emerging health technologies.
